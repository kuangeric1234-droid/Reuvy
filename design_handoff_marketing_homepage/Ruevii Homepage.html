AHPRA compliance

Compliance that works the way regulators expect

Cosmetic injectables are under real scrutiny in Australia. Ruevii bakes the guidelines into the workflow — so doing the right thing is simply how the software works.

01
Mandatory clinical gating

Treatments can’t be booked or charged until a valid consultation and consent exist on the record.

02
In-person & video consultations

Run compliant initial consults face-to-face or over secure telehealth, captured to the patient file.

03
S4 drug register

Every scheduled drug is logged against the patient, prescriber and batch automatically.

04
Immutable audit trail

Who did what, when — a tamper-evident log across the whole clinic, ready for any review.
